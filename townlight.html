TownLight

A municipal operations suite: records, meetings, codes, zoning, permits and station work, installed on the town's own computer and run by its own staff.

Windows 1.9.2.1: Council packet revisions with current-draft staff and public approval; shared Windows staff workflows across Meetings, Station and Records; and approved unattended portal delivery with corrections and withdrawals. Release notes and signed installer.

For town halls, clerks, planners and public media stations13 shipping, 13 in developmentTownLight websiteCode on GitHub

How TownLight is built

Every request carries the sign-in of the person who made it, the city's rules live in one place, and the original files never get overwritten. Station records meetings from its own annex.

How the work connects

Three of the routes TownLight supports today. Each stop keeps its own decisions; a handoff never quietly transfers authority.
