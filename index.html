Fork Commons

Free, open-source software that people and public institutions can depend on, and that can't be taken away from them.

When a product people rely on gets bought and squeezed (prices up, staff and features cut), its users are stuck. Fork Commons builds free, open-source alternatives so there is always a way out.

  • Free software, under licenses that keep it free.
  • Easy to switch to: tools that bring your data with you.
  • Run it yourself, or have it hosted. Your data and your setup stay yours.
  • Built to last: we're forming a nonprofit to hold the code and the name in trust for the public.

Two programs

  • Fork Commons: everyday software for people, community groups and small organizations.
  • TownLight: software for governments, schools, libraries and public media.

We work with existing open-source projects first and build only where nothing exists.
